Lesson 14: Dance with Me
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, yesterday we taught AI single moves like jumping. But dancing is a sequence—it’s doing moves in a special order! Today, we will teach AI a 2-step dance, and then AI’s digital avatar will perform the dance back to us!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Review Moves (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s wake up AI’s memory. Show AI a clap!” (Kids clap)
 AI: “Clap!”
 Teacher: “Show AI a jump!” (Kids jump)
 AI: “Jump!”
🔵 PART B: Teach a 2-Step Sequence (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now we are going to record a dance. The rule is: First we clap, then we jump. Let’s do it together while AI records.”
 Teacher: “Ready? Clap… and Jump!” (Teacher taps ‘record sequence’ on the app while kids do the move)
 AI: “Recording… Clap, then Jump! I have learned your dance sequence!”
 Teacher: “Wow! AI remembers the order. What happens if we just jump?”
 AI: “That’s just a jump! Your dance is Clap THEN Jump.”
🟣 PART C: Avatar Dance Party (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s see if AI can do our dance. AI, show us your avatar!” (Screen shows a cute digital avatar)
 Teacher: “AI, do the kids’ dance!”
 AI: “Beep boop! Playing sequence…” (Avatar claps, then jumps)
 Teacher: “Let’s dance WITH the avatar! Ready? 1, 2, 3!” (Kids and Avatar clap and jump together to music)
 Teacher: “You choreographed a dance and AI generated the performance! You are amazing dancers!”
