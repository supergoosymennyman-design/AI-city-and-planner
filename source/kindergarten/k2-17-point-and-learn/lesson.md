Lesson 17: Point & Learn
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, today we are going to teach AI about the human body! We have a picture of a body on the screen. We will use our fingers to point to parts like the head and toes, and AI will learn their names!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Head and Shoulders (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at the body diagram on the screen. I’m going to point to the top part. AI, what is this?”
 AI: “I don’t know that body part.”
 Teacher: “Friends, touch your head and tell AI!” All: (Touching heads) “AI, this is the head!”
 AI: “Head! I’m learning!” (Teacher points to shoulders. Kids touch shoulders and teach AI)
🔵 PART B: Knees and Toes (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s go down low. I’m pointing to the middle of the leg.”
 Children: “Knees!” All: “AI, this is the knees!”
 AI: “Knees! Good for bending!”
 Teacher: “Point to the very bottom! Toes! Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is the toes!”
 AI: “Toes! Ten little toes!”
🟣 PART C: AI Highlights, Kids Touch (9 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now AI will test you! AI is going to make a body part glow on the screen. You have to shout the name and touch it on your OWN body!”
 AI: (Head glows) “What is this?”
 Children: (Touching heads) “Head!”
 AI: (Knees glow) “What about this?”
 Children: (Touching knees) “Knees!”
 Teacher: “AI learned the body, and you know your bodies perfectly! Great job!”
