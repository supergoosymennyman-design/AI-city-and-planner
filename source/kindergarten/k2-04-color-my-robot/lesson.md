Lesson 4: Color My Robot
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, last time we taught AI about colors. Today, we are going to use our voices to tell AI how to color the robots we built! AI can listen to our words and generate a brand new, colorful robot just for us.”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Review Colors (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s wake up AI’s memory. AI, what color is the sun?”
 AI: “Yellow!”
 Teacher: “What color is the sky?”
 AI: “Blue!”
 Teacher: “What color is an apple?”
 AI: “Red! I remember my colors!”
🔵 PART B: Teacher Models Color Instructions (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now, let’s tell AI to color a robot. I will say: ‘Make my robot’s head red and its body blue.’” (Teacher uses voice-to-text and text-to-image)
 AI: “Beep boop! Coloring robot…” (Screen shows a robot with a red head and blue body)
 AI: “Here is your colorful robot!”
 Teacher: “Wow! AI listened to the colors and generated the picture! Let’s try one more together. What color should the arms be?”
 Children: “Yellow!”
 Teacher: “Say it with me: ‘AI, make the arms yellow!’” All: “AI, make the arms yellow!”
 AI: “Generating… Yellow arms added!”
🟣 PART C: Kids Color Robots & Quiz (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now it’s your turn! Tell the tablet what colors you want for your robot’s head, body, and legs.” (Kids give voice commands. AI generates their colored robots)
 Teacher: “Before we finish, AI has a quick color quiz for you! AI, ask a question!”
 AI: (Shows a red object) “What color is this?”
 Children: “Red!”
 AI: “Great job! You are amazing robot designers!”
