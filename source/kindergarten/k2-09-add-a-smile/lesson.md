Lesson 9: Add a Smile
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, last time we taught AI about feelings using cards. Today, we are going to draw faces ourselves to teach AI, and then we will tell AI to add those feelings to our robots! AI can generate faces on the robots we built.”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Draw and Generalize (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “I’m going to draw three different happy faces on the tablet. A big smile, a little smile, and a smile with teeth.” (Teacher draws them)
 Teacher: “AI, what feeling are all of these?”
 AI: “They are all… Happy! I can generalize!”
 Teacher: “Wow! AI learned that even if the smiles look a little different, they all mean ‘happy’!” (Teacher repeats quickly for sad and angry faces)
🔵 PART B: Make Robot Happy (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s bring our robot from Lesson 2 back. Look at this robot. It has no face! Let’s use our voice to tell AI what to do. Say: ‘Make robot happy!’” All: “Make robot happy!” (Teacher taps generate)
 AI: “Generating face… Beep boop!” (Screen shows the robot with a big happy smile)
 AI: “Your robot is happy!”
🟣 PART C: Kids Design Robot Faces (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Tell your tablet how your robot is feeling! Say ‘Make robot sad’ or ‘Make robot angry’ and watch AI draw the face!” (Kids give commands, AI generates the faces on their robots)
 Teacher: “Your robots have so much personality now! Great job teaching AI about emotions.”
