Lesson 16: Make a Beat
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, yesterday we recorded sounds. Today, we are going to be music producers! We will take our sounds and use buttons to arrange them into a 4-beat song. Then, AI will generate a real musical beat out of our arrangement!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Record 3 Sounds (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “We need 3 sounds for our song. Let’s record a table tap, a hand clap, and a ‘Hey!’” (Teacher quickly records the 3 sounds into the Song Maker tool buttons)
 AI: “Sound 1 saved! Sound 2 saved! Sound 3 saved!”
🔵 PART B: Arrange the Beat (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s arrange them into 4 beats. Let’s tap the buttons in this order: Tap, Clap, Tap, Hey!” (Teacher and kids tap the buttons on the smartboard/tablets in sequence)
 AI: “Arranging beat… Tap, Clap, Tap, Hey!”
 Teacher: “Now AI is going to generate a full song using our beat. Listen!”
 AI: (Generates a catchy, rhythmic musical track incorporating the kids’ sounds) “Here is your generated song!”
 Children: (Cheering and dancing)
🟣 PART C: Kids Make Their Own Beats (7 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now use your tablets! Pick your favorite sounds, press 4 buttons, and let AI generate your very own dance track!” (Kids press buttons. AI generates short beats for each)
 Teacher: “You didn’t just make a noise; you gave AI a pattern, and AI generated music! You are music producers!”
