Lesson 18: AI’s Turn to Lead
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, last time we taught AI body parts. Today, AI is going to be the gym teacher! AI will use its voice to give us movement commands, and the camera will watch to see if we follow the instructions correctly.”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Review Body Parts (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s show AI we remember. Touch your head!” (Kids touch head)
 AI: “I see heads!”
 Teacher: “Touch your toes!” (Kids touch toes)
 AI: “I see toes!”
🔵 PART B: AI Gives Commands (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now, stand up and listen to AI. AI, give the kids a command!”
 AI: “Okay! Touch your… knees!” (Kids touch knees. The joint recognition camera scans the room)
 AI: “Scanning… Yes! You are all touching your knees! Good job!”
 AI: “Next command: Touch your… shoulders!” (Kids touch shoulders)
 AI: “Scanning… Perfect shoulders!”
🟣 PART C: Speed Round & Freeze (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s make it harder! AI, give us commands fast, and when you say ‘Freeze’, we have to stop!”
 AI: “Touch head! … Touch toes! … Touch knees! … FREEZE!” (Kids freeze in silly positions)
 AI: (Scanning) “Wow, you are like statues! You followed my voice instructions perfectly!”
 Teacher: “AI used its voice to lead, and its camera to check. You were amazing followers!”
