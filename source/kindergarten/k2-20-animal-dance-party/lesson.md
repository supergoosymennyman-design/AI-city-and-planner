Lesson 20: Animal Dance Party
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, today is a party! We are going to combine everything we learned. We will pick an animal from our memory, pick a dance move, and tell AI to generate an animation of that animal doing that dance! AI will make a movie just for us!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Choose the Star (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s pick an animal for our movie. Should it be a bear, a frog, or a bird?”
 Children: “A frog!”
 Teacher: “Okay, our star is a frog! AI, bring up a frog!” (Screen shows a 3D or 2D frog model)
🔵 PART B: Choose the Dance & Generate (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now, what dance should the frog do? Should it do our 2-step dance (clap then jump) or just do silly frog jumps?”
 Children: “Silly frog jumps!”
 Teacher: “Okay, let’s give AI the command. Say: ‘AI, make the frog do silly jumps!’” All: “AI, make the frog do silly jumps!”
 Teacher: (Taps generate/animate)
 AI: “Generating animation… Beep boop!” (Screen shows the frog doing hilarious, bouncy jumps to music)
 Children: (Laughing)
🟣 PART C: The Dance Party (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s have an Animal Dance Party! AI, generate a bird flying, and a bear clapping!” (AI cycles through animated animals dancing on the screen)
 Teacher: “Everyone stand up and dance with the animals on the screen!” (Kids dance along with the AI-generated animations)
 Teacher: “You taught AI animals, you taught AI moves, and AI generated a dance party! You are AI Masters! Wave bye-bye to Phase 2!”
 AI: “Bye-bye, dancers! See you in Phase 3!”
