Lesson 8: AI’s Feeling Cards
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, humans have feelings like happy, sad, and angry. Today, we are going to teach AI about emotions using picture cards! AI will look at the faces on the cards and learn what happy, sad, and angry look like.”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Happy (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at these three cards. They all have big smiles. AI, do you know what feeling this is?”
 AI: “I don’t know.”
 Teacher: “Friends, what feeling is this?”
 Children: “Happy!”
 Teacher: “Let’s show AI three happy cards so it learns. Say: ‘AI, this is happy!’” All: “AI, this is happy!”
 AI: “Happy! I am learning!”
🔵 PART B: Teach Sad and Angry (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now look at these cards with tears. What feeling is this?”
 Children: “Sad!”
 Teacher: “Show AI the three sad cards. Say: ‘AI, this is sad!’” All: “AI, this is sad!”
 AI: “Sad! Oh no.”
 Teacher: “What about these cards with frowns and red faces?”
 Children: “Angry!” All: “AI, this is angry!”
 AI: “Angry! Grrr.”
🟣 PART C: Emotion Matching Game (9 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s play a game! AI is going to draw a face on the screen, and you have to guess the emotion and make that face yourselves!”
 AI: (Draws a simple smiley face) “What feeling is this?”
 Children: (Smiling) “Happy!”
 AI: “Correct!”
 AI: (Draws a frowning face) “What about this?”
 Children: (Frowning) “Angry!”
 Teacher: “You taught AI all about feelings! Give yourselves a happy smile!”
