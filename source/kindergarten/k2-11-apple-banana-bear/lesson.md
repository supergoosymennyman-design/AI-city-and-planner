Lesson 11: Apple, Banana, Bear
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, today we are going to use real toys to teach AI! AI usually looks at flat pictures on a screen, but today we will use the tablet camera to show AI our 3D toys. We will teach AI the names of our toys, and then AI will play a guessing game with us!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Fruits (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at this real toy apple. Let’s hold it up to the tablet camera. AI, what is this?”
 AI: “Hmm… I see a red circle, but I don’t know what toy it is.”
 Teacher: “Friends, what fruit is this?”
 Children: “Apple!”
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI. Say: ‘AI, this is an apple!’” All: “AI, this is an apple!”
 Teacher: (Taps ‘teach’ on the camera app) “Listen AI, this is an apple.”
 AI: “Apple! Yummy!” 🍎 (Repeat the exact same process for the toy banana)
🔵 PART B: Teach the Bear & Test (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s show AI our fuzzy friend. Hold up the toy bear. AI, what is this?”
 AI: “I don’t know this fuzzy thing.”
 Teacher: “Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is a bear!”
 AI: “Bear! Roar!” 🐻
 Teacher: “Now AI knows our toys. Let’s test AI! AI, ask the kids for a toy.”
 AI: “Okay! Show me… a banana!”
 Teacher: “Quick, find your toy banana and show it to the camera!” (Kids hold up bananas. AI scans via camera)
 AI: “I see it! That is a banana! Good job!”
🟣 PART C: Speed Show-and-Tell (9 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s do a speed round! AI, ask for them fast!”
 AI: “Show me… an apple! … Show me… a bear! … Show me… a banana!” (Kids scramble to hold up the correct toys to the camera)
 AI: “Wow, you are so fast! Thank you for teaching me your toys!”
 Teacher: “You taught AI how to see real objects through the camera. Amazing work!”
