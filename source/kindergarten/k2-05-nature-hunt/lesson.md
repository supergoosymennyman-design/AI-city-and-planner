Lesson 5: Nature Hunt
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, today we are going to teach AI about the beautiful things in nature! AI doesn’t know what a flower or a cloud is yet. We will use our tablet cameras to point at real photos, and AI will learn to recognize them!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Sun and Cloud (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at this photo of the sky. I’m going to point my finger at the sun on the screen. AI, what am I pointing at?”
 AI: “I don’t know what that bright thing is.”
 Teacher: “Friends, what is that?”
 Children: “The sun!”
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI. Say: ‘AI, this is the sun!’” All: “AI, this is the sun!”
 AI: “Sun! It’s so bright!” (Teacher points to a cloud in the photo)
 Teacher: “What about this fluffy thing?”
 Children: “Cloud!” All: “AI, this is a cloud!”
 AI: “Cloud! Fluffy and white!”
🔵 PART B: Teach Flower (7 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s look at this picture of a garden. I’m pointing to this beautiful plant with petals.”
 AI: “I don’t know.”
 Children: “Flower!”
 Teacher: “Teach AI!” All: “AI, this is a flower!”
 AI: “Flower! Smells good!”
🟣 PART C: Point and Learn Game (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now I’m going to show you a big nature picture on the board. Who wants to come up, point to a sun, cloud, or flower, and see if AI remembers?” (Kids take turns pointing to items on the smartboard/tablet)
 AI: “That’s a flower! I remember!” / “That’s a cloud!”
 Teacher: “AI learned so much about nature today because you were great pointers and teachers!”
