Lesson 2: Robot Builder
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, remember how we taught AI about shapes? Today, AI is going to use what it learned to build something amazing! AI can not only learn, but it can also create things, like generating a picture of a robot, just by listening to our instructions. Let’s be AI’s boss and tell it what to build!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Review Shapes (5 minutes)
  Teacher: “Let’s see if AI remembers our shapes from last time. AI, look at this!” (Draws a square)
  AI: “That’s a square! I remember!”
  Teacher: “Great job, AI! What about this?” (Draws a circle)
 AI: “Circle! Easy-peasy!”
 Teacher: “You are so smart, AI. Today, we are going to use shapes to build a robot. But AI needs our instructions to draw it.”
🔵 PART B: Teacher Models Instructions (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Watch how I give AI a 2-step command using my voice. AI, listen carefully: Draw a square body, and a circle head.” (Teacher taps voice-to-text, then text-to-image generation)
 AI: (Processing sound) “Beep boop! Generating robot…” (Screen shows a robot with a square body and circle head)
 AI: “Here is your robot! Square body, circle head!”
 Teacher: “Wow! Did you see that? AI listened to my words and generated a picture! Let’s try another one together. What shape should the hat be?”
 Children: “Triangle!”
 Teacher: “Okay, let’s say it together: ‘AI, draw a triangle hat!’” All: “AI, draw a triangle hat!”
 AI: “Generating… Here is your robot with a triangle hat!”
🟣 PART C: Kids Build Robots (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now it’s your turn! Think of two shapes for your robot. Tell your tablet what to draw!” (Kids use voice-to-text to give commands like “Circle tummy, square legs”)
 AI: (Responding to various kids) “Generating robot! Here is a circle tummy!”
 Teacher: “Look at all these amazing robots! AI learned our shapes and generated new pictures just for you. Give AI a round of applause!”
