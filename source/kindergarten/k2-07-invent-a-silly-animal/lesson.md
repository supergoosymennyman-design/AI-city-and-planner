Lesson 7: Invent a Silly Animal
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, AI can learn what real animals look like, but did you know AI can also imagine things that don’t exist? Today, we are going to invent silly, mixed-up animals, like a duck with cat ears! We will show AI our ideas, and AI will generate a real picture of our silly animal!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Duck and Cat (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “First, let’s make sure AI knows normal animals. Show AI your duck play-doh or drawing.”
 AI: “Duck!”
 Teacher: “Show AI your cat.”
 AI: “Cat! Meow!”
🔵 PART B: Invent the Hybrid (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now, let’s get silly! I want an animal that has a duck body, but cat ears. Let’s draw it or make it with play-doh.” (Teacher makes a quick silly hybrid)
 Teacher: “AI, look at this! This is a ‘Cat-Duck’!”
 AI: “Wow! A duck with cat ears! That is silly!”
 Teacher: “Now AI is going to use its imagination to generate a beautiful picture of our Cat-Duck.” (Teacher taps generate)
 AI: “Generating silly animal… Here is your Cat-Duck!” (Screen shows a cute generated image of a duck with cat ears)
 Children: “Wow!”
🟣 PART C: Kids Invent and Generate (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now it’s your turn! Think of two animals to mix together. Make it, tell AI what it is, and watch AI generate it!” (Kids create hybrids like ‘Dog-Bird’ or ‘Bear-Fish’)
 AI: (For each child) “Generating Bear-Fish… Here is your silly animal!”
 Teacher: “You used AI’s imagination to bring your silly ideas to life! Amazing!”
