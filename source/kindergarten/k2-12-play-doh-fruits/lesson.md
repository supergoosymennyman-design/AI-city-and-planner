Lesson 12: Play-Doh Fruits
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, yesterday AI learned what real toy fruits look like. But what if we make a fruit out of Play-Doh? Will AI still know what it is? Today, we are going to teach AI that a Play-Doh apple is STILL an apple! AI can learn from our 3D clay creations.”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Review Real Toys (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s warm up AI’s brain. Show AI your real toy apple.”
 AI: “Apple! I remember!”
 Teacher: “Show AI your real toy banana.”
 AI: “Banana! Yellow and yummy!”
🔵 PART B: Cross-Medium Learning (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now, look at this apple I made out of red Play-Doh. Let’s show it to the camera. AI, what is this?”
 AI: “Hmm… that looks like a red lump. I don’t know.”
 Teacher: “Oh! AI is confused because it’s clay! Friends, what did I make?”
 Children: “An apple!”
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI that Play-Doh can be an apple too! Say: ‘AI, this is a Play-Doh apple!’” All: “AI, this is a Play-Doh apple!”
 Teacher: (Taps teach/label) “Listen AI, clay can be an apple too.”
 AI: “Oh! A clay apple! I am learning that things can look different but still be an apple!” 🤯
🟣 PART C: Kids Create and Test (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now it’s your turn! Make a fruit or a bear out of Play-Doh. When you are done, hold it to the camera and see if AI remembers!” (Kids mold their creations and hold them up)
 AI: (Scanning) “That is a banana! … That is a bear! You are master sculptors!”
 Teacher: “AI learned that it doesn’t matter if it’s plastic or Play-Doh, an apple is an apple! Great teaching!”
