Lesson 13: Jump, AI, Jump!
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, AI has been looking at pictures and toys, but today AI is going to watch us move! We are going to use the camera to teach AI our body movements, like jumping and clapping. AI will watch our joints—our arms and legs—and learn what we are doing!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Stand and Clap (8 minutes) 
 Teacher: “Everyone stand up nice and tall! AI, look at our bodies. What are we doing?” 
 AI: “You are very still. I don’t know the word.” 
 Teacher: “We are standing! Say: ‘AI, this is standing!’” All: “AI, this is standing!” 
 AI: “Standing! Tall like a tree!” 
 Teacher: “Now give your hands a big clap! AI, what is this?” 
 AI: “Your hands are hitting together. I don’t know.” 
 Teacher: “Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is a clap!” 
 AI: “Clap! Clap! Good job!”
🔵 PART B: Teach Jump (8 minutes) 
 Teacher: “Now for the fun one. We need to show AI three times so it really learns. Ready? One, two, three, JUMP!” (Kids jump) 
 Teacher: “Again! JUMP!” (Kids jump) 
 Teacher: “One more! JUMP!” (Kids jump) 
 AI: “Whoa! You went up and down! Is that… a jump?” 
 Teacher: “Yes! Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is a jump!” 
 AI: “Jump! I love jumping!”
🟣 PART C: AI Calls the Moves (9 minutes) 
 Teacher: “Now AI is the boss. AI, tell the kids what to do, and watch them with your camera!” 
 AI: “Show me… a clap!” (Kids clap. 
 AI: “I see clapping!”) 
 AI: “Show me… a jump!” (Kids jump. 
 AI: “I see jumping!”) 
 AI: “Show me… standing!” (Kids freeze. 
 AI: “Perfect standing!”) 
 Teacher: “AI learned our moves just by watching our bodies! Give yourselves a clap!”
