Lesson 6: Stamp & Test
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, today we are playing with play-doh and stamps! We are going to teach AI what our stamps look like. Then, AI is going to play a game with us. AI will ask for a specific stamp, and you have to find it and show it to the camera!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach AI Stamps (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at this duck stamp. I’ll press it into the play-doh and show it to the camera. AI, what is this?”
 AI: “I don’t know.”
 Teacher: “What is it, friends?”
 Children: “A duck!”
 Teacher: “Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is a duck!”
 AI: “Duck! Quack quack!” (Teacher repeats for star and heart stamps)
🔵 PART B: AI Tests Students (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now AI knows our stamps. AI, it’s time to test the kids! Ask them for a stamp.”
 AI: “Okay! Show me a… star!”
 Teacher: “Quick, find the star stamp and show it to the tablet!” (Kids scramble to find the star stamp and hold it up)
 AI: (Scanning via camera) “I see it! That is a star! Good job!”
 AI: “Next… Show me a duck!” (Kids hold up duck stamps)
 AI: “Quack! That’s a duck!”
🟣 PART C: Speed Round (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s do a speed round! AI, ask for them as fast as you can!”
 AI: “Heart! … Duck! … Star!” (Kids hold up the correct stamps quickly)
 Teacher: “You are too fast for AI! Great job listening and showing!”
