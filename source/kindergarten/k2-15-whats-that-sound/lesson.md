Lesson 15: What’s That Sound?
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, AI has eyes to see, but today we are going to give AI ears! We will use the microphone to record our own sounds—like clapping or saying ‘wow’—and teach AI what they are called. Then, AI will play a sound, and you have to do the action!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Record and Label (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “I’m going to tap the red record button. Everyone, give me one loud clap!” (Teacher records)
 Teacher: “AI, what sound was that?”
 AI: “I heard a noise, but I don’t know the name.”
 Teacher: “Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is a clap!”
 AI: “Clap! I saved the sound!” (Teacher repeats for a loud foot stomp, and the kids saying “WOW!”)
🔵 PART B: Sound Guessing Game (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now AI is going to play a sound from its memory. Listen carefully and do the action!”
 AI: (Plays the recorded stomp sound) “What sound is this?”
 Children: (Stomping feet) “Stomp!”
 AI: “Correct! You are great listeners.”
 AI: (Plays the “WOW” sound) “What about this?”
 Children: (Throwing hands up) “WOW!”
🟣 PART C: AI Repeats the Beat (7 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s make AI say our sounds back in a silly rhythm! AI, play: Clap, Stomp, Wow!”
 AI: (Plays back the kids’ actual recorded voices/sounds in order) “Clap! Stomp! WOW!”
 Teacher: “We taught AI to listen, remember, and play back our exact sounds! Give AI a big WOW!”
