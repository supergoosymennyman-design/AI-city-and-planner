Lesson 19: Charades with AI
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, do you know the game Charades? It’s when you act like an animal without speaking, and someone guesses! Today, we will teach AI animal movements. Then, YOU will act out an animal, and AI will use its camera to guess what animal you are!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Frog and Bird (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI how a frog moves. Everyone, get down low and do a big frog jump!” (Kids do frog jumps 3 times while AI records the joint movements)
 Teacher: “AI, what animal moves like this?”
 AI: “You went low and jumped… Is it a frog?”
 Teacher: “Yes! Say: ‘AI, this is a frog jump!’” All: “AI, this is a frog jump!”
 AI: “Frog jump! Ribbit!” (Teacher repeats the process for flapping arms like a bird flying)
🔵 PART B: AI Guesses the Teacher (5 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now I am going to act, and AI has to guess. Watch me!” (Teacher flaps arms and runs around)
 AI: (Scanning joints) “Arms are flapping… You are a bird!”
 Teacher: “AI got it! AI can recognize our movements!”
🟣 PART C: Kids Play Charades (10 minutes)
 Teacher: “Who wants to come to the front and act out an animal for AI to guess?” (A student comes up and does a heavy, stomping bear walk or a frog jump)
 AI: (Scanning) “Heavy steps… Are you a bear?”
 Children: “Yes!”
 AI: “I guessed it! You are great actors!”
 Teacher: “AI connected your body movements to the animals in its library! Brilliant!”
