Lesson 3: Color the Rainbow
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A) “Friends, AI knows shapes, but right now, AI sees everything in black and white! Today, we are going to teach AI about colors. We will use our tablets to color shapes, and AI will learn the names of the colors. Then, AI will be able to see the rainbow just like us!”
⏱️ 25-Minute Script 🟢 PART A: Teach Red (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Look at this empty circle on the screen. I’m going to color it with the red crayon tool. Watch.” (Teacher colors the circle red)
 Teacher: “AI, what color is this?”
 AI: “Hmm… I don’t know this color.”
 Teacher: “Oh! AI doesn’t know red. Friends, what color is this?”
 Children: “Red!”
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI. Say: ‘AI, this is red!’” All: “AI, this is red!”
 Teacher: (Taps ‘teach’ or labels it) “Listen AI, this is red.”
 AI: “Red! Like an apple! I’m learning!”
🔵 PART B: Teach Blue and Yellow (8 minutes)
 Teacher: “Let’s teach AI another color. I’ll color this square blue. What color is it?”
 Children: “Blue!”
 Teacher: “Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is blue!”
 AI: “Blue! Like the sky!”
 Teacher: “One more. Let’s color this triangle yellow. Tell AI!” All: “AI, this is yellow!”
 AI: “Yellow! Like the sun! I know three colors now!”
🟣 PART C: Color Recognition Game (9 minutes)
 Teacher: “Now let’s play a game! AI is going to show us a color, and you have to shout out its name. Ready?”
 AI: (Flashes a red screen) “What color is this?”
 Children: “Red!”
 AI: “Correct! You are so smart!” (AI flashes blue, then yellow. Kids shout the answers)
 Teacher: “You taught AI so well, and now you are color experts! Next time, we will use colors to paint a robot!”
