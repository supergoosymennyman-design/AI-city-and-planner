Lesson 10: Simon Says, AI Says
🎯 Simple AI Explanation (Say in Part A)
"Friends, today we are going to teach AI how to count using our fingers! We will show AI 1 to 5 fingers, and AI will learn the numbers. Then, AI is going to play 'Simon Says' with us and give us commands!"
⏱️ 25-Minute Script (Teacher Says → Kids Respond → AI Responds via App/Voice)
🟢 PART A: Teach Numbers 1-3 (8 minutes)
Teacher: "Hold up one finger! AI, what number is this?"
AI: "I don't know."
Teacher: "Tell AI!" All: "AI, this is one!"
AI: "One!" (Teacher and kids repeat for 2 and 3 fingers)
🔵 PART B: Teach Numbers 4-5 (7 minutes)
Teacher: "Now show AI four fingers. Tell AI!" All: "AI, this is four!"
AI: "Four!"
Teacher: "Show me a whole hand! Five fingers! Tell AI!" All: "AI, this is five!"
AI: "Five! High five!"
🟣 PART C: AI Plays Simon Says (10 minutes)
Teacher: "Now AI knows numbers 1 to 5. Let's play 'AI Says'! If AI says 'AI says show me 3 fingers', you do it. If AI doesn't say 'AI says', don't do it! Ready?"
AI: "AI says… show me 2 fingers!" (Kids hold up 2 fingers. AI scans with camera)
AI: "I see 2 fingers! Good job!"
AI: "Show me 5 fingers!" (Some kids might hold up 5)
Teacher: "Uh oh! Did AI say 'AI says'?"
Children: "No!"
Teacher: "Put them down! You have to listen carefully! Let's play again!"
AI: "AI says… drag the red box to square 1!" (Kids do the drag-and-drop on their tablets)
Teacher: "You are great listeners and great number teachers!"
💡 Teacher Tips for Lesson 10
App suggestions: Use a camera-based finger counting detection app, or pre-record AI responses for each number. If no camera AI is available, the teacher can simulate AI responses.
If AI "counts wrong": "AI is still learning! Let's show it again slowly."
Let children take turns being "Simon" — the one who gives commands.
No internet? Pre-record AI voice responses on the tablet and play them at the right moments.
