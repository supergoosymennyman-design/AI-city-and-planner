This is Bo — the AI you teach.

A friendly little screen-robot. Its screen is its face, so children can read how it feels — and it boots up knowing nothing. Everything it learns shows on its memory shelf. It's clearly a machine (the face is on a display), so kids bond with it without thinking it's a person who already knows things.

Why this works
  • A face to read: emoji-style screen = emotion any 4-yr-old gets, no words.
  • Honest machine: face is on a display; Bo boots up blank and learns from the child.
  • Visible memory: the shelf shows it only knows what you taught (Big Idea 3).
  • Consistent + offline: our own SVG faces — identical on every device, no CDN.
  • Reusable: 6 faces + the body = every game, no new art per lesson.
