# Recycling Sorter and Driving School: Workshop–City handoff

Use this guide when adding these activities to another Passiona folder. It describes the current source contracts and student journey; adapt the routes, assets and storage to the destination folder rather than copying paths unchanged. The canonical implementation is under [`buddy-kit/client`](../../buddy-kit/client/). The [P5 agent guide](../../AGENTS.md) applies to changes there.

## Student journey

**Recycling.** In the Workshop **Recycling** tab, the child teaches a Model block with material examples and connects a City intake, routing blocks and destination bins. **Save sorter** validates and publishes a frozen graph. **Try in AI City** saves again, then opens the recycling station with the selected exercise. The default **Material practice** has twelve held-out objects across metal, plastic, cardboard and glass. **My sorting photos** accepts up to sixty images for the current browser session; City reports destinations and counts without an answer-key accuracy score. **Earlier practice** keeps the nine-object set. In City, Start, Pause, Step and Reset control a trial. The graph decides the destination, including human check; the visible parcel reaches that destination before bin counts advance. **Edit in Workshop** returns to the saved machine. Saving changed teaching or wiring and resetting the City trial runs the newly published graph; unsaved edits leave the previous City graph in place. See [Workshop controls](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/sorter-panel.js), [graph validation and runner](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/logic/recycling-machine.js), [publication](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/recycling-machine-project.js), [exercise choice](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/recycling-exercises.js), and [City activity](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/city-activities.js).

**Driving.** In the Workshop **Driving** tab, the child explicitly teaches two Data-sense models: **steering** and **speed**. **Try in AI City** saves the machine and publishes the pair in one project-store transaction. The Audi school opens on a mixed journey by default; the child can choose a focused drill or preview eligible routes on their own City roads. The two models independently choose steering and target speed from simulated sensor readings. A completed journey requires a stopped arrival inside the finish area without a violation or intervention. After a run, the child can inspect a decision, choose **Improve this decision**, correct one model in Workshop, publish again, then repeat the same conditions with the installed revision. **Replay saved decisions** retains the original recorded revision; **Repeat with installed revision** runs the saved scenario again with the current pair. See [teaching UI](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/driving-teacher.js), [pair publication](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/publish-capability.js), [bundle and revision store](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/driving-project.js), [simulation](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/driving-simulation.js), [City launcher and evidence](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/driving-arena.js), and [route admission](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/driving-routes.js).

## Integration map

```text
Workshop tabs + saved machine ── publish ──> active project in IndexedDB
  Recycling: validated graph ──────────────> projects.recyclingMachine
  Driving: steering + speed bundle ─────────> projects.driving.bundles / installed
                     │ same origin, active project ID
                     ▼
City ?activity=recycling ── graph runner ──> projects.cityActivities.recycling
City ?activity=driving ──── paired Audi ────> projects.driving.attempts
                     │
                     ▼
Complete .passiona Champion File ── restore as a new local project
```

| --- | --- |
| Workshop selection and launch | [`skill-tabs.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/skill-tabs.js) keeps separate Recycling, Driving and Free build machine IDs in `projects.workshopSkills`, flushes workspaces before a tab change or launch, and routes to `../city-builder/?activity=…`. Recycling's launch is in [`sorter-panel.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/sorter-panel.js); driving's is in the tabs. A failed save blocks navigation. |
| Project and revisions | [`project-store.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/project-store.js) owns the active project, IndexedDB sections and mutations. [`recycling-machine-project.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/recycling-machine-project.js) increments the graph revision only when its snapshot changes and keeps the old published graph if saving fails. [`driving-project.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/driving-project.js) installs both models atomically, retains older bundles, and reuses an identical prior revision. |
| City entry and runtimes | [`city-builder.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/city-builder.js) routes `activity=recycling` to [`city-activities.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/city-activities.js) and normal `activity=driving` to [`driving-arena.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/driving-arena.js). Recycling executes the saved graph through [`recycling-machine-host.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/recycling-machine-host.js); driving prepares the installed bundle and uses [`driving-simulation.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/driving-simulation.js). The school can use its placed City site or a separate venue when no site fits. |
| Results and recovery | Graded recycling summaries and selected exercise live in `projects.cityActivities` and `projects.activityExercises`; personal-photo runs are session-only. Driving keeps up to three completed attempts in `projects.driving.attempts`, with scenario, revision and decision evidence. [`backup-coordinator.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/backup-coordinator.js) flushes and captures Workshop/City state into the complete `.passiona` archive and restores it as a new active project. The older [`champion-file.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/champion-file.js) format is accepted for migration, but its localStorage payload is not the complete project archive. Cloud City codes are smaller snapshots, not a substitute for that archive. |

Keep Workshop, City and the [project hub](../../buddy-kit/client/project-hub/hub.js) on one browser origin so they share the active-project marker and storage. The [personal-photo session](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/sorter-session.js) is keyed by project ID and preprocessing version; feature vectors, quick models and waiting photos use `sessionStorage`. Only explicitly saved classifier capabilities in `projects.sorterModels`, the published recycling graph, teaching work and paired driving bundles travel in the complete archive. Original uploaded photos do not. City must not quietly choose another classifier if an explicit reference is missing or incompatible. The current primary recycling run reads `projects.recyclingMachine`; the selection resolver remains for older classifier flows.

## Current path and compatibility path

The primary recycling experience executes the **whole Workshop machine**: intake, trained Model blocks, gates, routing and bins. The earlier image-classifier capability and direct conveyor path remain in [`publish-capability.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/publish-capability.js), [`recycling.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/recycling.js), and [`sorter-session.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/sorter-session.js) for saved installations. Do not replace graph execution with a single classifier prediction. The v1 nine-object dataset and [`city-waste.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/city-waste.js) also remain; new material practice uses [`city-waste-v2.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-common/city-waste-v2.js) and the [v2 catalogue](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/assets/city-recycling-v2/catalogue.js). Exercise selection is independent of model teaching: opening v2 must not retrain an old machine.

The primary driving experience is the **steering and speed pair** in `projects.driving`. The earlier single Number-sense `.cap` model and `bend`/`obstacle`/`light` school live behind **Earlier driving machine** and explicit legacy URL handling in [`skill-tabs.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/skill-tabs.js), [`publish-capability.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/workshop/publish-capability.js), and [`city-activities.js`](../../buddy-kit/client/city-builder/city-activities.js). Keep their stored results and entry routes readable, but use the paired Audi path for new student work. Unversioned saved school scenarios also use the separate compatibility venue; current course-version trials use the placed school when it fits.

## Ordered adaptation checklist

1. **Establish the origin and project boundary.** Serve Workshop, City and project hub under one origin; carry the active-project ID, project-store schema, workspace flush and switch/import events. Preserve per-tab machine IDs. Close or invalidate an open trial when the project changes; never show another project's machine or evidence.
2. **Bring the assets and loaders across.** Serve both recycling catalogues and their scanner/object assets, the Audi GLB, and the City library references used by the station. Use [`createGLTFLoader`](../../buddy-kit/client/shared/gltf.js) for GLBs so Draco and Meshopt decoding works. Keep the placed school reservation and its no-space venue fallback; report a failed Audi or scanner load and allow retry.
3. **Implement publication before navigation.** For recycling, save the editable machine, reject unsupported or incomplete graphs, freeze the graph, and publish its changed revision atomically. For driving, require both taught models, export their actual examples and thresholds, validate the bundle and install the pair in one transaction. Reuse unchanged revisions; never overwrite an older recorded revision. Propagate save errors to the child.
4. **Wire the launch and trial lifecycle.** Carry `activity` and exercise parameters from Workshop into City. For recycling, read the published graph and selected dataset, clone the trial input, and reread the graph on Reset; do not swap models mid-run. For driving, prepare the installed pair when setting up a trial, keep its scenario and revision fixed while running, and release temporary scene objects, camera changes and traffic reservations on exit, route change, hidden page, context loss or project switch. Keep Pause, Step, repeat and replay distinct.
5. **Handle absence and incompatibility visibly.** Missing graph or pair, stale personal-photo session, incompatible classifier, invalid route, absent space, asset failure and failed result save each need a clear state and a way back to Workshop or Planner. Missing inputs must not silently fall back to another model. Keep human check distinct from a correct material bin and do not score personal photos against an answer key.
6. **Preserve results and recovery.** Save graded recycling results with dataset version and exercise choice; retain driving attempts with exact scenario and model revision. Export a complete Champion File after workspace flush, verify its required assets, restore as a separate project, reload, and confirm the selected machine, published revisions and saved attempts are usable. Keep session-only photo features out of the archive.
7. **Verify the full round trip.** Teach, publish, launch, finish and inspect one recycling practice and one paired driving trial; return to Workshop, change a prediction, republish and rerun the same conditions. Exercise the missing/incompatible model screens, old compatibility entries, no-space and asset failures, project switching during a trial, and complete `.passiona` restore. Start with [`recycling-machine` tests](../../../tests/recycling-machine.test.mjs), [`driving-pair` tests](../../../tests/driving-pair.test.mjs), and the [Workshop skills](../../tests/e2e/workshop-skills.spec.mjs), [recycling](../../tests/e2e/recycling.spec.mjs), and [driving pair](../../tests/e2e/driving-pair.spec.mjs) browser flows; adapt assertions to the destination folder. For this repo, run `npm run test:unit` and focused Playwright against the built City bundle. This document alone does not require an app build.
