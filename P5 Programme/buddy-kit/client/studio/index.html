      How to use 3D Studio

      How rigging works

      A skeleton is a chain of joints. Tap the model in Rig mode to place the first joint; every next tap adds a joint linked to the selected one. Tap a ball to select it, so the next tap branches from there. Drag a ball to move it — turn the model and drag again to set its depth. The bending is worked out for you a moment after you stop. Joints belong to the shape you tapped: move, turn or scale the shape and they come along. A shape with no joint inside rides the nearest bone.

      In Pose mode, tap a ball. The ring appears at the joint above it — that is the hinge. Turn the ring to swing the lit piece. The first ball turns the whole model. Rest straightens everything; Export saves a .glb with the skeleton, and importing that file brings the skeleton back.

      Directions

      The FRONT arrow points toward +Z — build your character facing that way. Red = X (right), green = Y (up), blue = Z (forward).

      Modes

      Build (1) — add shapes and move them around.

      Rig (2) — tap the model to place joints; drag balls to move them.

      Pose (3) — tap a ball and turn the ring at the joint above it to swing the lit piece.

      Fit (4) — load a gear model (GLB / OBJ / FBX), pick a slot, auto-fit it onto the current character, then adjust it with Move / Rotate / Scale and download the result.

      Hotkeys

      Delete / Backspace — delete the selected shape · Ctrl+D — duplicate · Ctrl+Z — undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y — redo

      G / R / S — move / rotate / scale gizmo · 1-4 — switch modes

      In Scale, turn on Keep proportions to resize an object without changing its shape.

      Tips

      Move several shapes at once: Shift-click to select a few shapes, then drag the gizmo — they all move, spin, or scale together around one shared centre. Ctrl+D duplicates the whole selection.

      Shape sculpting: select a shape, then press 🧱 Sculpt in its Details to open the Clay Studio with just that shape. Pull, Push, Smooth, Grab, Carve and Paint it, with Mirror and Undo. Press ✅ Done to put the sculpted shape back in your scene.

      How to use the Clay Studio

      This is the shape you picked — sculpt it with your finger, like playdough!

      One finger on the clay sculpts it with the chosen tool. One finger on the background turns the shape around. Two fingers (or pinch) zoom.

      Pull / Push — pinch the surface in or out. Smooth — soften bumps. Grab — drag a lump around. Carve — scratch a thin line. Paint — add colour that stays glued to the shape.

      Mirror sculpts both sides at once. Brush sets the tool size. Undo steps back one stroke. ✅ Done puts the sculpted shape back in your scene; ✕ Cancel throws the sculpt away.
