# 3D Asset Attribution — Champion City (champion-city)

External GLB models used by the procedural champion city, with licenses and sources.

## Park Trees
Files: `tree.glb`, `tree-high.glb`

- **Source:** [Kenney Mini Forest](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-forest)
- **Creator:** Kenney (kenney.nl)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 (Public Domain) — free for personal, educational and
  commercial use; no attribution required.
- **Notes:** Low-poly stylized trees with a shared colormap texture. Loaded
  async in `city.js`; parks keep the fast procedural spheres until the GLB
  models arrive, then each tree is swapped in-place for a real low-poly tree.
  The colormap texture lives at `assets/models/Textures/colormap.png`.

## Reuse rules
- If a model is removed or replaced, update this file.
- Kenney CC0 models are public domain — attribution kept here for provenance.
