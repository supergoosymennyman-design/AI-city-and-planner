# PLAN: AI City Architect — P3-P4 Capstone

## Concept
A grand 20×20 city builder sandbox integrating concepts from all 17 AI literacy lessons. Students scan land, build infrastructure, configure AI systems, build causal graphs, and simulate a living city with moving agents.

## Phases
1. **Design**: Scan (3 hazards) → Build (22 types, 6 systems) → Roads (drag network)
2. **Optimise**: Knowledge graph (causal chains) → AI analysis → 6 scenarios + 3 HILT
3. **Simulate**: Buses/waste trucks/drones on routes → Weather cycles → 3 crises

## AI Concepts Integrated
Waste (L1), Subsurface scanning (L2), Grid movement (L3), Drone routing (L4-L5), Delivery loops (L6), Bus scheduling (L7), Healthcare (L8), Water (L9), Power (L10), Monitoring (L11), Traffic (L12-L13), Air traffic (L14), Tokenomics (L15), Sentiment (L16), Government (L17)

## Scoring
⭐ 6+ buildings placed / ⭐ 5+ graph edges / ⭐ 3 HILT decisions / ⭐ 2+ crises handled
