# AI City Architect — P3-P4 (Ages 8-9)

Design a 20×20 smart city with 22 building types, road network pathfinding, agent simulation (buses, waste trucks, drones), causal knowledge graph, and 3 black swan crisis events.

## How to Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome. Best on tablet in landscape.

### Phase 1: Design
- 🔍 **Scan** the land first — find 3 hidden hazards (Turbo drag or Normal click)
- 🏗️ **Place buildings** from 6 system categories
- 🛣️ **Draw roads** by clicking tiles — they form a network
- ⚙️ **Configure** buildings with sliders (bus frequency, staff allocation, etc.)
- 🪙 Budget: 150 tokens

### Phase 2: Optimise
- 🔗 **Knowledge graph** — connect buildings with causal relationships
- 🔍 **AI Analysis** — find feedback loops and bottlenecks
- 🎯 **6 Scenarios** — traffic, power, waste, bus, drone, water
- 👤 **Human-in-the-Loop** — moral trade-off decisions

### Phase 3: Simulate
- 🚌 **Buses** move on road routes
- 🚚 **Waste trucks** on collection loops
- 🛸 **Drones** between pads
- ☀️→🌧️ **Weather cycles** affect operations
- 🔥 **Crises**: Heatwave, Flood, Storm
- ⏱️ **Speed controls**: 1×, 10×, 100×

### Quick Start
Press "⚡ Quick Start" on the intro screen to jump straight to a 60+ building city with pre-built routes, ready for optimisation and simulation.

## Tech
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. 16 files. Dark dashboard theme. Web Speech + Web Audio APIs.
