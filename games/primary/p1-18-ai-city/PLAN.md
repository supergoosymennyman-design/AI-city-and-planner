# PLAN: My First AI City — P1-P2 (Ages 6-7)

## Game Concept
A tactile, cheerful city-building game where children drag colorful buildings onto a 6×6 grid, connect them with utilities, and watch their city come alive.

## AI Narrative Arc
Nova Jr. is a friendly robot who learns alongside the child. Each time the child places a new building type, Nova Jr. "learns" what it does. By the end, Nova Jr. understands what a city needs to function.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Build (15 min)
- 6×6 grid with river terrain
- 6 building types from palette
- Subsurface scanning uncovers hidden hazards
- Budget: 20 coins
- Minimum: 3 homes, 1 school, 1 hospital, 1 water, 1 power

### Phase 2: Connect (10 min)
- Water pipes (blue), Power lines (yellow), Roads (purple)
- Tap source → tap target to connect
- Bonus waste sorting mini-game

### Phase 3: Watch (10 min)
- Citizens fade in/out at buildings
- Bus loops along road connections
- Rainstorm crisis: tap 3 puddles to drain
- Speed controls: Slow/Normal/Fast

## AI Concepts Used
| Concept | Original Lesson | How It Appears |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Waste Classification | L1 | Recycling mini-game bonus |
| Subsurface Scanning | L2 | Scan tiles for hidden hazards |
| Grid Movement | L3 | Roads connect buildings |
| Water Supply | L9 | Pipe connections from water tower |
| Power Grid | L10 | Wire connections from power plant |
| Sentiment | L16 | Building mood indicators |

## Scoring
- ⭐ All minimum buildings placed
- ⭐ All buildings connected
- ⭐ All puddles drained

## Tech
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, Web Speech API, Web Audio API. Tablet-first.
