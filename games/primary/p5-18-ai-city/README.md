# AI City Architect — P5-P6 (Ages 10-11)

Design a complex smart city, build causal knowledge graphs, manage 4 cascading crises, and observe your city evolve over simulated decades.

## How to Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome. Best on tablet in landscape.

### Phase 1: Design
- 15×15 grid with 6 systems: Power, Water, Transport, Health, Waste, Governance
- 20+ building types including drones, data centers, and schools
- Advanced subsurface scanning with 3 limited drill samples
- Layer toggles: Power/Utility, Logistics/Waste, Social/Safety
- Visual scripting editor for drones and buses
- Budget: 150 tokens

### Phase 2: Optimize
- Free-form knowledge graph with directed causal arrows
- Find feedback loops for bonus points
- Run AI analysis to detect design flaws
- Human-in-the-Loop (HILT) scenarios: moral decisions with no perfect answer

### Phase 3: Simulate
- Full agent-based simulation with 50+ agents
- 3 speed modes: 1× (inspect), 1,000× (daily), 1,000,000× (years)
- 4 cascading black swan events: Heatwave+Cyber, Flood, Economic Shock, Population Surge
- Economic dashboard: GDP, inflation, debt, approval
- Time progression shows demographic shifts and aging infrastructure

## Tech
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. Dark dashboard theme. Web Speech + Web Audio APIs.
